# Night Noodling — YouTube backing tracks

Meditative improv, no sheet music. Slow, atmospheric, minor-key jams. Favor
"in the style of" originals over exact-song backing tracks: real songs have
changes your ear expects you to hit, style jams just loop a mood.

Anchor track: [Comfortably Numb Jam — Pink Floyd Style (B minor)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8lsxsFAd9ew) (Now YOU Shred)
