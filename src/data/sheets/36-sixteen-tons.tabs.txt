Sixteen Tons — Tennessee Ernie Ford (1955 Capitol single)
Track: hand written rhythm adaptation, charted in A minor
Tuning (high→low): E B G D A E — 4/4, swing the eighths, about 140 bpm
Legend: x muted strum . palm mute h hammer-on

guitar part to copy, and the only Songsterr transcription of the Ford single is AI
generated and pedals E all the way through. So this is a swing comping part built
on the song's changes, not a transcription.
